How do I get an App Password?
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account (Google Account → Security).
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and create one named "Denise Mail".
  3. Copy the 16-letter code Google shows you and paste it above.

Work or school accounts may not allow App Passwords. If so, try the demo, or ask your admin.
